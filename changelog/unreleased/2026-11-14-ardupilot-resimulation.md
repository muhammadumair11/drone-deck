- **Drone Deck: ArduPilot flights re-simulate.** A recording of a simulated ArduPilot system with ROS 2, an ArduPilot flight log (`.bin`, read by a new DataFlash reader) or a drone's `/ap/` MCAP flies again in a fresh ArduPilot SITL, which runs on a ROS domain of its own (80) with its own wiped state folder, since its DDS client takes no namespace; the command player joins that domain to the run's, replays `/ap/cmd_vel`, `/ap/cmd_gps_pose` and `/ap/joy`, and calls arm, mode switch and takeoff where the recorded `/ap/status` changed (services a recording cannot keep), serving the same services to your code in shadow. Takeover, the comparison (ArduPilot's pose turned to north, east, down; Copter mode names) and the parameter transfer rules (ArduPilot's DDS, system id, frame and hardware parameters stay out) cover ArduPilot. None of it has run against a real ArduPilot (VAL-22).

- **Drone Deck: ArduPilot systems get a working ROS 2 bridge.** With the ROS agent on, the launch starts ArduPilot's own DDS Agent (`dds-ap`, UDP 2019) and passes the system's ROS domain to SITL's DDS client (`DDS_DOMAIN_ID` from a defaults file), and setup installs **ArduPilot ROS 2 (DDS) support** (SITL rebuilt with `--enable-DDS` through Micro-XRCE-DDS-Gen v4.5.1, and `ardupilot_msgs`) instead of `px4_msgs`. `profiles.json` schema 5 adds the new built-ins and gives unedited ArduPilot commands the `DRONE_DECK_AP_*` variables; edited commands are kept. Recorders and the command player now source the PX4 and ArduPilot message workspaces when they exist, and ordinary replay also drops ArduPilot's `/ap/cmd_*` and `/ap/joy` command topics.
